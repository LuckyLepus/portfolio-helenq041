## Service 01 — AI Creative Workflow Consulting

**适用于 / Best for:** Creative agencies, brand teams, independent creative studios

### What it is
Designing end-to-end AI creative workflows tailored to your team's output type, tools, and creative standards. This is not "what tools to use" advice — it's system design for creative AI adoption.

### What you get
- Current workflow audit (how your team creates today)
- AI tool mapping (which tools solve which creative problems)
- SOP documentation (step-by-step playbooks for repeatable AI-augmented workflows)
- Prompt library design (reusable system prompts for your brand/style)
- Training & handoff (your team knows how to run it independently)

### Scope examples
- Midjourney/SD visual system for a brand event creative team
- LLM-based pitch deck research and draft automation
- ComfyUI workflow for 3D-to-2D brand visual pipeline
- Multi-tool creative content pipeline for editorial teams

### Format
- Project-based (4–12 weeks)
- Retainer (ongoing system evolution)

## Service 02 — Creative SOP Design & Documentation

**适用于 / Best for:** Teams scaling AI adoption; studios building IP; organizations with complex creative output

### What it is
Translating institutional creative knowledge and AI workflows into structured, executable SOP documentation. Combines process design with knowledge capture.

### What you get
- Existing process documentation (capture what your team does, even informally)
- AI-augmented workflow SOPs (how AI fits each stage)
- Role-specific playbooks (what does each team member need to know)
- Knowledge base structure design (so your SOP is findable and usable)

### Format
- Project-based (3–8 weeks)
- Can be combined with Service 01

## Service 03 — AI Tool Adoption Strategy

**适用于 / Best for:** Creative directors, studio heads, brand managers deciding where to invest in AI

### What it is
Strategic advisory on which AI tools to adopt, how to sequence adoption, and how to manage the cultural change of AI integration in creative teams.

### What you get
- Tool landscape mapping (what's available, what's stable, what's appropriate for your context)
- Adoption roadmap (phased approach to avoid disruption)
- Risk/quality framework (where AI helps vs. where human judgment must lead)
- Team change management guidance

### Format
- Workshop (1–3 sessions)
- Strategic advisory retainer

## Collaboration Principles

1. **Context before advice:** Every engagement starts with understanding the actual situation — not deploying a generic template.
2. **Honest about AI limitations:** HelenQ will tell you where AI doesn't help or creates new problems, not just where it's exciting.
3. **Executable over theoretical:** Deliverables are things you can actually use, not decks with frameworks.
4. **Appropriate scope:** Projects are scoped for realistic outcomes — no overpromising.
